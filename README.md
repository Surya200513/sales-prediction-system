# sales-prediction-system
The Sales Prediction System is a Power BI and Excel analytics project that estimates monthly soap demand, sales value, and profit across 30 areas (with a combined population of 2,373,000 and 263,667 average soap bars/month at ₹26 per soap and ₹5.50 profit per soap). It also breaks down per-soap cost components (₹20.50 total cost price vs. ₹26 selling price and ₹5.50 profit), compares competitor soap prices and scores, and presents the data across a 10-page interactive Power BI report with KPI cards, slicers, navigation buttons, and charts.   Short Job Description (JD) — Sales Prediction System (Power BI Project)Role: Power BI / Data Analyst (Academic / Entry-Level Project)Overview:Build and analyse interactive Power BI dashboards for a Sales Prediction System to estimate area-wise monthly soap demand, revenue, and profit alongside unit cost breakdowns and brand comparisons.   Key Responsibilities:Clean, model, and analyse area-wise population, monthly soap volume, pricing, cost components, and brand scoring data in Excel and Power BI.   Create multi-page Power BI reports featuring KPI cards, slicers, action buttons, and charts to compare regional demand, unit economics, and brand rankings.   Support sales planning and pricing/cost reviews using visual summaries of projected volumes, revenue, and profit across locations.
